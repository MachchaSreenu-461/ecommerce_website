A better cup starts with better leaves.

At Pattari, we believe tea should taste like the place it came from. That is why we work directly with small family farms and select each harvest carefully. From fragrant green teas to warming spice blends, every product is packed in small batches to preserve its natural character.

No unnecessary processing. No forgotten harvests sitting on shelves. Just fresh ingredients, honest sourcing, and a cup worth slowing down for.

Our philosophy

Why the dates matter

Tea changes with time. Its aroma and character gradually fade after harvest, which is why we make the harvest date visible on every tin. Instead of wondering how old your tea is, you can simply look at the label.

From farm to cup

Where it comes from

Our ingredients come from family farms across growing regions known for their distinctive tea and spices. We work closely with growers and select each batch based on freshness, aroma, and quality.
