About Pattari

A tea shop that tells you the harvest date, on purpose.

Pattari started as a way to buy tea the way we buy vegetables — knowing where it grew and how recently it was picked.

How it started

Pattari began with four farms we kept visiting long after we needed to — two in the Nilgiris, one in Darjeeling, one in Idukki. Every one of them dated their pickings by hand. We just carried that habit onto the tin.

What we do differently

No blending house, no year-old stock sold as "fresh." Each batch is roasted or dried within days of picking, packed in small runs, and listed with its actual harvest window — not a marketing season.

Frequently asked

How fresh is "fresh"?

Most tins reach you within 2–4 months of harvest. The exact picking date is printed on the base of every tin.

Do you ship outside India?

Not yet — this demo store only handles domestic delivery for now.

Can I return a tin once opened?

Unopened tins can be returned within 7 days. Once opened, we can't resell it, so we're unable to accept a return.

How should I store loose leaf tea?

Keep it airtight, away from light and heat. The tin itself is fine for this — no need to transfer it.
